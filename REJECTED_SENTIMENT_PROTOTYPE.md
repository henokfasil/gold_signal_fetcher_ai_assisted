# Rejected sentiment prototype

The untracked July 2026 sentiment prototype is not part of the validated
research system and must not be deployed or cited as completed evidence.

The reviewed prototype did not implement its claimed point-in-time news or
sentiment sources. One benchmark generated placeholder random metrics, while
another reused SMC price features as a proxy for sentiment. Those approaches
cannot support a causal sentiment claim, model promotion, paper approval, or
Telegram behavior.

The original untracked files are preserved locally under
`.local_archive/rejected_sentiment_20260720/` for audit only. They are excluded
from Git and runtime paths.

Any future sentiment experiment requires a new pre-registered contract with
identified point-in-time sources, deterministic collectors, timestamp and
revision provenance, missing-data behavior, chronological evaluation, frozen
promotion gates, and tests proving isolation from decisions until every gate
passes. Placeholder or randomly generated evaluation values are prohibited.
